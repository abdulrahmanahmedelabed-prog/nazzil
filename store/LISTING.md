## Full description (EN)
Nazzil turns a link into a ready file in one tap.

• Paste a link, share it to Nazzil from any app, or browse a platform inside the app and tap “Download with Nazzil”.
• MP3 and M4A audio; MP4 and WEBM video up to 4K.
• Preview before downloading: thumbnail, title, duration.
• Parallel download queue with pause, resume and retry that keeps going in the background.
• Add-ons: trim a clip, whole playlists, embedded metadata and subtitles, SponsorBlock, and your choice of audio cover — the video thumbnail, a frame from the video, or your own image.
• Arabic and English, dark and light themes.
• Privacy first: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Only download content you own or have permission to download, and follow each platform's terms.

## Google Play — Data safety
- Data collected: **None**. Data shared: **None**.
- Data is processed on-device only; nothing is sent to the developer.
- Encryption in transit: downloads use the sites' own HTTPS.
- Users can delete their data: history/settings are cleared in-app or by uninstalling.

## Google Play — Foreground service declaration
- Type: **dataSync**. Use: continuing user-started downloads (and converting/saving them) when the user leaves the app, with an ongoing progress notification. The service stops as soon as the queue is empty.

## Google Play — Content rating questionnaire
- No violence, sexual content, gambling, user-to-user communication or location sharing in the app itself. The app accesses the web (built-in browser) → answer "Yes" to "unrestricted internet access".
